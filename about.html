About

A home for every hobby.

merava started with a simple frustration: trying something new — a pottery class, a climbing gym, a Saturday cooking workshop — meant hunting across a dozen different sites, sign-up sheets, and phone calls. We wanted one place to discover it all instead.

Today merava connects people to local studios across every kind of hobby, and gives those studios simple tools to reach new students and manage their bookings. No subscriptions to juggle, no spreadsheets, just one credit system that works everywhere.

We're a small team building for curious people and the local businesses that make trying something new possible.
